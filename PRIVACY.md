# Privacy

CipherNest's current release is local-only. It does not require an account and does not intentionally send vault contents, identifiers, analytics, or telemetry to a CipherNest service.

The operating system, app store, crash facilities, clipboard manager, backups configured by the device owner, or third-party accessibility/input software may independently process data according to their own policies. CipherNest cannot control a compromised operating system.

Diagnostic exports must remain redacted and must never contain decrypted vault records or keys.
